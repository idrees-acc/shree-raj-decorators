Fifteen years of Malad's celebrations

Shree Raj Decorators opened at Shop 13, Shree Amizara Society, near Kashmiri Tailor, in 2012. We began with mandaps and lighting, and families kept calling us back for the next birthday, puja and naamkaran.

Every job still starts the same way: we see the venue, listen to what the family wants, and plan the decoration around it.
